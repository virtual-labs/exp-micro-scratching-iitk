### Procedure
Open the Micro Scratching Simulation

Select the material specimen for the experiment.<br>
Set the scratching parameters such as:<br>
Applied load<br>
Scratch length<br>
Scratching velocity<br>
Click on the Start/Play button to begin the simulation.<br>
Observe the movement of the indenter over the specimen surface.<br>
Monitor the scratch formation and deformation on the material surface.<br>
Note the values of scratch depth, friction force, and other displayed outputs.<br>
Record the observations shown in the graphs/results section.<br>
Repeat the simulation with different loading conditions and compare the scratching behavior.
